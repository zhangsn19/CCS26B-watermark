The following is the interview script. Please note that we framed the question as non-leading and open-ended when asking participants.

1: General Usage and Conceptualization

- Q1: What generative AI tools have you interacted with so far?

- Q2: In what specific scenarios or for what types of tasks do you typically use these tools?

- Q3: Prior to this study, what was your understanding of how digital content is traced or identified ``behind the scenes''?

- Q4: How was your perception of invisible watermarking evolved during this study, and what factors influenced that shift?

2. Contextual Privacy and Trust Boundaries

- Q5: In your opinion, what types of information should be embedded in watermarks, and what is the rationale behind your answer?

- Q6: Which entities (e.g., organizations or individuals) do you identify as potential parties that could access watermark data? Why?

- Q7: What do you think are the potential privacy concerns and why? Could you elaborate on the answers in your questionnaire? (If applicable) Could you provide some examples that watermarks bring privacy concerns?

- Q8: Are there scenarios where you would find watermarking highly beneficial, and where you would find it intrusive?

- Q9: What factors determine your level of comfort when different entities (e.g., platforms, governments, employers) access embedded data?

- Q10: How would that acceptance change when you are a content creator, or when you are a viewer?

- Q11: How does different information types embedded (e.g., creation time vs. user ID) change your perspective on data collection?

- Q12: How does the interaction between the category of embedded data and the accessing entity influence your privacy concerns? (e.g., why might an DI be acceptable for one entity but not another?)

3. Perceived Impacts and Trade-offs

- Q13: If a piece of your original work were incorrectly identified as AI-generated, what you would concern?

- Q14: How you would weigh watermark's technical robustness (anti-tampering), output quality and privacy?

- Q15: How do you perceive the relationship between traceability and personal privacy? Do you believe there is an inherent tension between them, and why?

4. Accountability and Transparency

- Q16: In your opinion, what level of notification or ``right to know'' should a user have when an AI tool applies an invisible watermark to their prompt or output?

- Q17: How would you prefer relevant stakeholders to notify you regarding the collection and usage of such watermarking data?

- Q18: Should there be an option for users to opt-out of identity-linked watermarking, and what would be the implications of such as choice?

- Q19: What specific mechanisms, either technical or societal, would you advocate for to better protect your privacy?

- Q20: Are there any other thoughts that you would like to share?
